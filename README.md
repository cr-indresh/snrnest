# SNR NEST — Corporate Website

> **"Building Careers. Empowering Businesses. Creating Opportunities."**

SNR NEST is a premier multi-service corporate ecosystem connecting **Talent + Businesses + Technology + Education + Careers**.

---

## 🚀 Five Core Business Verticals

1. **BPO Hiring** (`bpo-hiring.html`)
   - Voice & Non-Voice Process recruitment
   - Customer Support & Back Office operations
   - Fast-track 5-step screening to onboarding pipeline
   - Partner ecosystem: Tata Elxsi, Concentrix, KreditBee, Stable Money, Axis Bank

2. **Manpower Sourcing** (`manpower-sourcing.html`)
   - Dark Store & Quick Commerce operational staffing
   - Picking & Packing operations, Shift Supervisors, Warehouse Team Leads
   - Rapid 48-72 hour deployment with 100% statutory compliance

3. **Website Solutions** (`website-solutions.html`)
   - High-impact corporate platforms & high-converting landing pages
   - Modern Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS engineering
   - Technical SEO, schema markup, and monthly SLA maintenance

4. **Internship Programs** (`internship.html`)
   - Industry-aligned student cohorts in HR, Talent Acquisition, Operations & Digital Marketing
   - Structured visual pathway: Internship → Live Project → Certificate → Career Launch

5. **Training & Placement** (`training-placement.html`)
   - Intensive bootcamps in Full Stack, AI & ML, Python Data Analytics, and BPO Leadership
   - 3-stage visual: Technical Training → Career Prep → Corporate Placement
   - **Coming Soon: We Are Expanding** section featuring Cloud & DevOps, Generative AI, and FinTech Risk

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Markup & Structure**: Semantic HTML5 with Schema.org meta tags & OpenGraph cards
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS + Custom Design System (`css/styles.css`)
- **Typography**: Google Fonts (*Outfit*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *JetBrains Mono*)
- **Icons**: Lucide Icons
- **Logic & Interactivity**: Modular Vanilla JavaScript (`js/app.js`, `js/data.js`, `js/jobs.js`, `js/internships.js`, `js/courses.js`)
- **Data Persistence**: Client-side LocalStorage mock API ready for backend integration
